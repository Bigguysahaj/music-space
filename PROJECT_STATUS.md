## Known issue: lyrics dropped when a score is supplied (2026-09-29, diagnosed, not fixed)

User report: with a melody score and Balanced/Melodic singing, not all lyrics are sung. Two stacked causes, from logs `remote-1219c297…` (transcribe) and `remote-7f5c0023…` (generate):
1. **Score shorter than lyrics.** The transcribed clip was 7.2 s, giving 16 notes. The Krishna mantra is ~33 syllables (9/8/8/8). With `cot` melody/full the model realizes the supplied `abc`, and YuE ABC carries no `w:` lyric alignment, so the model places syllables itself and drops the overflow. Rhythmic/spoken (`cot=off`) ignores the score, which is why it doesn't show this.
2. **Duration cap.** The generate job used duration 20 s and logged `Semantic song 0: 500 tokens (truncated)`, i.e. it was cut at the cap mid-song, not ended by the model. At 93 BPM the four lines need ~25–35 s.
Minor: a transcription of a real recording keeps any instrumental intro as leading rests, which spend time before the vocal enters.
Workaround: transcribe the full tune for all four lines, and set Length to 30–40 s when using a score. **Warnings built (2026-09-29):** a non-blocking "Melody check" (`cloudflare/public/assets/melody-check.js` + cards in `index.html`) under the score box, with a summary chip above Create music. Checks: too few notes for the lyrics' syllables (animated syllable→note picture), Length shorter than the last note (animated timeline, one-tap "Set Length"), Length far past the score (tip, suppressed when the score is too short anyway), late vocal entry (one-tap "Trim the intro" that only drops whole rest bars and only when every voice rests there), score ignored in Rhythmic mode (one-tap "Switch to Balanced"). Syllable counts are heuristic (Romanized vowel groups, `y` as consonant; Devanagari aksharas). Side finding: `requests/achyutam-mantra-v1.json`'s line 2 has 7 notes for 8 syllables. **Added:** when `V: Vocal` is all rests but `V: Ins` has notes (seen on a real 11 s hummed clip, job `00ba03db`; two 5 s clips had no melody in either voice), a "tune landed in the instrument part" card with one-tap "Use the instrument melody as the vocal", which swaps each Vocal block's music with the Ins block that follows it (bars and header unchanged). Timing checks are skipped when there are no sung notes.
